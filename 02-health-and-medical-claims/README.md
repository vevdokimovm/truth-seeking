# 02 — Health & Medical Claims

Alternative-medicine and "detox" protocols circulating on social media and in commercial products — mostly around "spike protein / vaccine detox", heavy-metal chelation, and cancer. Each row is a claim **under evaluation**; see [`../00-method`](../00-method/) for how.

> Note: several of these protocols recommend prescription drugs (antiparasitics, antimalarials, colchicine) off-label, plus chelating agents, at doses with real toxicity. Everything here is catalogued as a claim to assess — **not** as guidance. Raw protocol documents live in [`sources/`](sources/) as study material only; dosing is deliberately not re-typeset into a usable how-to.

## Claims index

| Claim | Source | Type | Status (initial — verify) |
|-------|--------|------|---------------------------|
| Nattokinase / bromelain / curcumin "dissolve" persistent spike protein | McCullough "BSD" protocol; TWC "Ultimate Spike Detox" (commercial) | Supplement + repurposed-drug protocol | Unsupported for the stated indication; no controlled evidence that spike protein persists and is cleared this way |
| Off-label antiparasitic / antimalarial add-ons for "spike" symptoms | McCullough protocol | Prescription drugs, off-label | Unsupported for this use; known toxicity — assess as a claim only |
| Zeolite / shungite "structured water" / activated charcoal / pine-needle tea detox | "awakened soul" (IG) | Binder / water protocol | Unsupported; "structured water" is not a recognised phenomenon |
| Shilajit + EDTA chelation to remove "graphene oxide" / nanotech | decodehealth (IG); comment threads | Chelation claim | Unsupported; the premise ("graphene oxide in vaccines") is itself unevidenced |
| Nicotine patches "dissolve nanotech" / "neutralise venoms in vaccines" | comment thread | Fringe claim | Unsupported; no proposed mechanism |
| "Biovitae" tea reduces vaccine inflammation | @moessance4life (IG) | Commercial product | Unsupported; marketing claim |

## To verify next

1. Trace the McCullough "BSD" manuscript to its actual publication and peer-review status.
2. For each ingredient, separate any *real* evidence for *some other* indication (e.g. bromelain as a digestive enzyme) from the "spike detox" claim specifically — don't let a genuine minor use launder the big claim.
3. Product stats ("71% report lower spike levels", "77% report more energy") — find the underlying data, if any exists, and check how "spike level" is even being measured.

## Alternative cancer therapy claims

Separate cluster (source: `sources/Cancer_саммари.pdf`). Same rule — catalogued as claims **under evaluation**, not guidance. Extra caution flag below.

> **Danger flag:** several of these are promoted as *replacements* for oncology, and some are directly toxic — MMS/chlorine dioxide (industrial bleach), laetrile (releases cyanide), megadose baking soda, prolonged fasting "instead of" treatment. Documented here to be evaluated and understood, **never** to be followed. The most harmful claim in this whole folder is the framing "do this *instead* of real cancer treatment."

| Claim | Source | Status (initial — verify) |
|-------|--------|---------------------------|
| Cancer is caused by parasites; cure with herbs + "zapper" device | Hulda Clark | Unsupported; no evidence parasites cause cancer; the zapper is pseudoscience |
| Amygdalin / laetrile / "B17" / apricot kernels induce apoptosis | comment threads, Saleem 2018 review | Unsupported in humans; **releases cyanide**, linked to poisoning cases; banned 1987; no clinical anticancer evidence |
| Baking soda "alkalinizes the body" and starves acidic tumors | Anane, comments | Unsupported; blood pH is tightly regulated (you can't diet it); dangerous in quantity |
| MMS / chlorine dioxide cures cancer | Jim Humble | Unsupported; **industrial bleach**; FDA warnings; ingestion causes serious harm |
| Repurposed drugs (ivermectin / fenbendazole / mebendazole) cure cancer — "12 mechanisms each" | Joe Tippens, IvermectinCure | **Mixed/overstated** — mechanisms are mostly in vitro / animal; preclinical ≠ clinical (>90% fail translation); not established human therapies. See `../01-how-science-fails/02-systemic-flaws.md` |
| Rife frequencies / Royal Rife machine destroy cancer cells | spooky2rife, M83heals | Unsupported; no clinical evidence base |
| Metabolic / ketogenic therapy "starves" cancer (Warburg effect) | Anane, Seyfried | **Mixed** — the Warburg effect is real biology and keto is under legitimate *adjunct* research (e.g. glioblastoma trials); the "cure / replace treatment" claim overreaches badly |
| Essiac tea, soursop, prolonged water fasting, TP53/elephants framing | comments | Unsupported; anecdote-level |

## To verify next (cancer)

1. For repurposed drugs and keto — pull the *actual* clinical trials (not in vitro), and mark exactly where real research ends and the "cure" claim begins. This is the folder's sharpest test of the method.
2. For each "banned / suppressed" framing (laetrile etc.) — check whether the ban was corruption or an actual safety/efficacy verdict. Don't assume either; read the record.
