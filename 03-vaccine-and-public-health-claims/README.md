# 03 — Vaccine & Public-Health Claims

Claims about vaccines, autism, and public health, mostly from social media (source: `sources/autizm-sammari.pdf`). Evaluated via [`../00-method`](../00-method/). This folder is a **strong test of the method**: real peer-reviewed studies sit right next to false claims, and the whole skill is telling apart what the real research actually shows from what it's *claimed* to show.

## Claims index

## Why this folder matters

The Amish allergy studies are the lesson in miniature. Real, solid, peer-reviewed science (farm microbial exposure lowers allergy risk) gets **misused** as if it supported a totally different, false claim (vaccines cause autism / vaccines are why the Amish are healthy). The method here isn't "reject everything the post says" — it's: go to the actual JACI/NEJM papers, see what they *actually* measured, and mark precisely where the real finding ends and the hijacking begins.
