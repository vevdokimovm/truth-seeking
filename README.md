**A critical-thinking workbench.** This repository exists for one purpose: to practice evaluating claims the way an independent researcher would — take a claim, strip it to its testable core, trace it to its primary source, weigh it against real evidence, and reach a conclusion I can defend. Nothing here is believed by default. **Inclusion means "under examination", never "endorsed."**

The stance is deliberate and explicit: **independent scientific researcher.** Not a believer collecting confirmations, not a cynic collecting dismissals — someone applying a consistent method to whatever comes in, and following the evidence wherever it lands.

> Working notes are in Russian; source material is mixed (RU / EN).

## The point of this repo

Two rules sit under everything here, and they cut in both directions:

1. **A label is not an argument.** Calling something a "conspiracy theory" settles nothing — and neither does calling something "science." The claim still has to be checked on its own merits. A true thing stays true no matter who mocks it; a false thing stays false no matter how many people repeat it.
2. **Volume and authority are not evidence.** "Millions swear by it" and "an expert said so" are both weak on their own. What counts is the primary source and the quality of the evidence behind it.

So every claim gets the *same* treatment, regardless of how it feels: state it precisely → find the original source → grade the evidence → check the mechanism → name the red flags → form a view with a confidence level attached. The method is the product. The claim files are just where it gets applied.

## Highlights

- **[`00-method`](00-method/)** — the toolkit: philosophy, epistemology, scientific reasoning, evidence hierarchy, finding & reading sources, and the statistics needed to check claims. **Read this first** — everything else is this method in action.
- **[`01-how-science-fails`](01-how-science-fails/)** — the core. How science itself gets corrupted (funding, fraud), breaks down (the replication crisis, p-hacking), and stays provisional (physics has been confidently wrong, and admits it). The foundation of scepticism toward *everything else*.
- **[`02-health-and-medical-claims`](02-health-and-medical-claims/)** — "detox", alternative cancer therapy, supplements, repurposed drugs, frequency medicine.
- **[`03-vaccine-and-public-health-claims`](03-vaccine-and-public-health-claims/)** — vaccine/autism narratives, the Amish, measles/outbreak claims. A strong test case: real peer-reviewed studies sit right next to false claims here.
- **[`04-frequency-and-sound-claims`](04-frequency-and-sound-claims/)** — solfeggio frequencies, brainwave entrainment, "organ frequencies", consciousness maps.
- **[`05-conspiracy-and-media-narratives`](05-conspiracy-and-media-narratives/)** — cultural / media conspiracy claims (industry "rituals", calendar theories, etc.).
- **[`06-document-investigations`](06-document-investigations/)** — primary-source document releases (e.g. Epstein files): reading what the records actually contain vs. what is claimed about them.
- **[`07-conspiracy-catalog`](07-conspiracy-catalog/)** — a register of every conspiracy claim I intend to examine, graded up front: `documented` / `mixed` / `unsupported` / `unfalsifiable` / `not-empirical`. Proven corporate cover-ups sit in the same table as flat-earth — deliberately, because only the method separates them, not intuition.
- **[`research-agenda.md`](research-agenda.md)** — the eight areas I actually dig into, and why.

## Data & privacy note

Repository is **private**. It reproduces third-party claims, marketing copy, and public records strictly for study and criticism, **not endorsement**. No personal or sensitive data is stored here; anything personal belongs in `health-vault` or `self-map` instead.

Some source material describes protocols involving prescription drugs and toxic substances. It is catalogued here as **claims to evaluate — never as guidance**; dosing is deliberately not re-typeset into usable instructions.

**Верстак критического мышления.** Репа существует ради одного: тренировать оценку утверждений так, как это делает независимый исследователь — взять claim, свести к проверяемому ядру, дойти до первоисточника, взвесить по реальным доказательствам и прийти к выводу, который можно защитить.
