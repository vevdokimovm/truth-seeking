# 06 — Document Investigations

Different kind of work from folders 01–02. Here the material is **primary-source document releases** — court filings, released records — not pseudoscience claims. The task is reading what the documents actually contain and separating sourced fact from the interpretation layered on top of them online.

## Method for this folder

1. Work from the actual released documents, not summaries or threads about them.
2. For any specific assertion, find the page / filing it supposedly comes from.
3. Tag each item clearly: **stated in the document** vs. **alleged** vs. **inference** vs. **unsupported claim circulating around it**.
4. Keep the distinctions sharp: *named in a document* ≠ *accused* ≠ *charged* ≠ *convicted*. These get collapsed constantly, and collapsing them is where most of the nonsense comes from.
