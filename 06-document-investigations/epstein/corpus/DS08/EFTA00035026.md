announced over the BA radio. I senior officer specialist 
responded to 9South (Special Housing Unit) L-tier 
cell #220, upon arrival I witnessed medical staff performing CPR on a unresponsive inmate (Epstein, Jeffrey

2nd floor Health Service Area as medical staff continuously perform CPR on inmate Epstein until relieved by E.M.S 
staff who continue administering CPR while escorting Epstein from the medical room to the rear gate area then 
placing him in to the EMT ambulance with Bop staff escort as I followed in a secure government vehicle to the 
Downtown Beckman hospital where I stayed on post until I was relieved.
