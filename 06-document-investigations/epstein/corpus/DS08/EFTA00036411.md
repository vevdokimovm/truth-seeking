Reminder of procedures in place. 
1) Each Wednesday, the D/W Operations Lieutenant will fill overtime for the following Sunday utilizing the overtime sign up 
list. Once the overtime sign up list has been exhausted any remaining vacancies can be filled with anyone that volunteers. If 
the D/W Lieutenant is not able to complete filling Sunday's overtime vacancies, during their shift on Wednesday, the E/W 
Operations Lieutenant will finish whatever is remaining. 
2) Each Sunday, the D/W Operations Lieutenant will fill all overtime vacancies for the week (Monday thru Saturday) utilizing 
the overtime sign up list. Once the overtime sign up list has been exhausted any remaining vacancies can be filled with 
anyone that volunteers. If the D/W Lieutenant is not able to complete filling overtime vacancies for the week, on Sunday, the 
E/W Operations Lieutenant will finish whatever is remaining. 
3) On Saturday and Sunday, PRIOR TO MANDATING STAFF, the Operations Lieutenant on each shift will have the Control 
Center send a message via the Ops Planner to all staff announcing there is overtime available. The announcement will only 
be done once, per shift, and if there are no volunteers after the announcement is made you will begin mandating staff.
