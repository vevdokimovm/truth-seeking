To Whom it May Concern: 
We are in receipt of Amazon's responses to the attached grand jury subpoena (the "Subpoena"), which were submitted 
several weeks past the return due date. 
We note with concern that the Subpoena responses list more than 300 descriptions of responsive materials as 
"REDACTED." Please immediately produce either a complete response to the Subpoena without the redaction of non-
privileged responsive materials, or provide a legal basis for your refusal to do so, so that we may seek court intervention 
as necessary. 
We further note that Amazon has explicitly reserved objections including that the information and/or records requested 
are unusually voluminous in nature and that compliance would cause an undue burden for Amazon. Please advise 
immediately whether Amazon is in fact exercising any of these objections in its response to this Subpoena specifically; 
that is, whether Amazon has failed to produce responsive materials based on volume or burden. Please also advise 
whether Amazon has refused to provide any responsive materials for any reason, other than the "REDACTED" materials as 
described above. 
Finally, we note that the Subpoena requested any and all records associated with nine email addresses, and the response 
appears to include materials in connection with just two email addresses. Please confirm that Amazon comprehensively 
reviewed its records for each of the other seven email accounts and found no responsive materials. Similarly, the 
Subpoena requested account notes and logs, including any customer service communications or other correspondence. 
No such records were produced. Please confirm that Amazon comprehensively reviewed its records for any such 
materials and that none were found.
