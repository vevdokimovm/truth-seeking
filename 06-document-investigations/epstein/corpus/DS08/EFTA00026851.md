ORDER 
This matter having come before the Court pursuant to an application under Title 20, 
United States Code, Section 1232g(b)(1)(1)(i), and Title 28, United States Code, Section 1651 
which application requests the issuance of an order directing Interlochen Center for the Arts 
("Interlochen") and its employees, located at 4000 Highway M-137 Interlochen, MI 49643, and 
Interlochen employees, not to notify any other person of the existence of the attached subpoena 
seeking records, the Court finds that the applicant has offered reasonable grounds to believe that 
such disclosure will result in the intimidation of potential witnesses, or the destruction of or 
tampering with evidence, and will seriously jeopardize the investigation. 
IT APPEARING that the information sought in the attached subpoena is directed at 
establishing the illegal activity under investigation and identifying the individuals responsible for 
such activity, there is reason to believe that disclosure of the scope and direction of the inquiry 
will result in the intimidation of potential witnesses, destruction of or tampering with evidence, 
and will seriously jeopardize the investigation. 
IT IS ORDERED that pursuant to Title 20, United States Code, Sectiott 1232g(bXl)Q i), 
and Title 28, United States Code, Section 1651, Interlochen and its employees shall not disclose 
the existence of the attached subpoena to any other person (except as necessary to carry out the 
subpoena) for a period of 180 days from the date.of this Order.
