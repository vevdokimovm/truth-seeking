Prosecution Agreement ("NPA") signed by Jeffrey Epstein on September 24, 2007.

a. All communications between the government — including, but not limited to, 
attorneys and staff at the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of 
Florida, the United States Attorney's Office for Southern District of New 
York, the Department of Justice, state prosecutor's offices, the FBI, and any 
other federal and state investigative agencies — and Mr. Epstein's attorneys.

including, but not limited to, attorneys and staff at the U.S. Attorney's Office

Southern District of New York, the Department of Justice, state prosecutor's 
offices, the FBI, and any other federal and state investigative agencies. 
c. Unredacted copies of all emails and other correspondence between the 
government and Mr. Epstein's attorneys concerning the negotiation of the 
NPA, previously produced by the government on August 13, 2020. See, e.g., 
SDNY GM 00134069 et seq.
